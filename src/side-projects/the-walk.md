Theme for 2023 GMTK Game Jam was "Roles Reversed". Our take on the theme was to create a world where dogs walk humans.
Gameplay is very simple as we wanted to focus mostly on evoking emotions. We are very happy with how this one turned
out. Most people who got to the end seem to be surprised by the ending and felt things.

You can play the game on PC from browser. Try giving it a shot!
